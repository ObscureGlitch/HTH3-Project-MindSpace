# Varied generated wall art

Three of the room's four framed botanical motifs are replaced with original
generated portrait prints while the larger centre botanical remains unchanged.

- **Quiet Orbit** uses interlocking leaf and crescent forms in forest, sage,
  burnt sienna, ochre and charcoal.
- **Still Water** uses broad teal, blue-green, sand and rust shapes that suggest
  a quiet lake without becoming a literal landscape.
- **Balanced Stones** uses rounded geometric forms, a sun disc and a curved line
  in terracotta, ochre, olive, clay rose and charcoal.

All three images were generated with the built-in image-generation tool as
portrait game textures. They contain no text, signatures, frames or room mockups.
The installer preserves the existing oak frames and paper mounts, fits each image
without changing its aspect ratio, and disables rather than deletes the original
clay motifs. It does not enter Play mode or start a bake.

The final prompts requested calm, flat, paper-textured modern abstract art in the
room's existing earthy palette, with large silhouettes that remain legible at the
small in-game display size.
