# Therapy room material sources

The realism pass uses the following Poly Haven CC0 texture sets at 2K resolution. Only diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness maps were downloaded; checksums were verified against the provider's API. The roughness maps are converted locally to Unity's smoothness-in-alpha convention.

- [Oak Veneer 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/oak_veneer_01): wood furniture and floor boards.
- [Curly Teddy Natural](https://polyhaven.com/a/curly_teddy_natural): cream upholstered seating.
- [Rough Linen](https://polyhaven.com/a/rough_linen): normal and roughness detail for pillows, curtains and lamp shades; their original solid fabric colors are retained.
- [Hessian 230](https://polyhaven.com/a/hessian_230): rug and woven baskets.
- [White Plaster 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/white_plaster_02): plaster and ceramic microstructure.
- [Knitted Fleece](https://polyhaven.com/a/knitted_fleece): sofa throw.

[Poly Haven license](https://polyhaven.com/license): CC0. No external service is called by the running game.
