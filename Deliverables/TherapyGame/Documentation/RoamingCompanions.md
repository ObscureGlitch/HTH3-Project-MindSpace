# Roaming companions

Only the companion selected at startup is present in the world. Either Julien or Camille starts at the same safe position inside the room and waits to talk. The unselected model, movement, animations and colliders are disabled. Choosing **Enable voice & begin** on the initial screen starts the selected companion's conversation while you are in the room; **Play without voice** never opens a microphone session. Later, press **E** while looking at them to open their controls and talk. If you explicitly change the companion in the menu later, the previous model is hidden before the new one appears indoors; both are never active together.

The companion cannot leave while you are inside. You must remain outside continuously for **more than five seconds**, and the door must be open far enough to pass through. Returning inside resets that timer. Menus, lost focus and paused gameplay do not count toward the wait. Closing the door blocks departure immediately, including during the closing animation; the bot cannot reopen it. This rule also applies to **Follow me**. A bot already outside is not teleported indoors: it cannot cross or reopen a closed door and must wait for the player to open it before returning through the doorway.

Open **V → Companions** to choose **Follow me**, **Stay here**, or **Explore** for your selected character. Following works with voice disabled. The follower starts catching up beyond 3 m and stops inside 1.9 m, slightly off to one side. They can continue a conversation while walking. Staying or exploring can be selected at any time.

The supplied `Walk` clip follows actual movement speed; stationary conversation clips and real-time vowel lip-sync resume when stopped. Only the selected companion receives conversation gestures. Roaming destinations are visited in a fixed sequence, with different starting points for each character.

Navigation is baked from the live level's solid colliders, with a 0.29 m agent radius, 1.9 m clearance, 35-degree slope limit, and 0.22 m steps. Companions use dry routes around the pond and remain inside the fenced map. Runtime capsule sweeps protect against the player and moving door, which checks the active companion's collider throughout its swing. Only the player operates the door. Unreachable destinations cause waiting or another exploration choice; following never teleports a character.

Conversations use a 6 m distance from the companion's current head position and require a clear line through the level. Audio follows the moving character. A wall or closed door interrupts hearing. After the initial consented indoor conversation, press E or the Talk button to request conversation. Passing walkers do not trigger new conversations. Existing mute, pause, consent and session limits remain in effect.

The installer backs up the open scene, checks follow hysteresis and pause decisions, and verifies complete paths between every exploration destination, including the room-to-garden doorway connection. `Characters/Roaming/RoamingCheck.txt` records installation results. `Deliverables/CheckCompanionRoaming.ps1` compiles runtime and editor code against the installed Unity dependencies.

`Deliverables/CheckCompanionRoomPolicy.ps1` runs device-free tests against the actual room policy: startup wait, strict five-second threshold, reset on return, nonaccumulating trips, paused time, closed/closing/open door states, and consent-only initial conversation.

After Unity refreshes in Edit mode, the queued validation includes the indoor return destination and checks all 132 directed routes among 12 stops, actor/door references, walking clips, and voice-off/mute/pause behavior without opening the microphone. Its result is recorded in `Characters/Roaming/RoamingValidation.txt`. It can also be run from **Therapy Game → Verify Roaming Companions**. Runtime movement still needs a Play-mode check; a source compilation or route audit alone does not confirm it.
