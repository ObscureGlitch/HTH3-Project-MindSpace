# Screenshot-based thumbnails

Only the transparent title was generated with the built-in image-generation tool. The three screenshot backgrounds are the user's original files, not regenerated imagery.

Title prompt:

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: transparent PNG title overlay for real screenshots of a cozy low-poly Unity game. Generate ONLY the lettering; do not generate or edit any scenery.
Text (verbatim): "MindSpace" exactly once, single line, capital M and capital S, all other letters lowercase, spelled M-i-n-d-S-p-a-c-e.
Style: polished, friendly, modern rounded sans-serif game title. Bold but not inflated bubble letters, smooth softened terminals, excellent thumbnail readability, natural kerning. Warm ivory faces with a narrow dark forest-green outline and a restrained soft dark-green drop shadow immediately around and below the letters, giving elegant subtle depth. No large offset extrusion, no cartoon sticker border.
Composition: very wide horizontal wordmark, fully visible, centered with modest transparent padding, no cropped letters.
Background: genuinely transparent alpha, including all spaces and the holes inside letters.
Constraints: title lettering and its tight shadow ONLY. No scenery, panel, box, underline, leaves, stars, icons, badges, tagline, second text, gradients outside letters, or watermark. This overlay will be placed on untouched screenshots; keep it clean and readable against both blue sky and dark aurora scenery.
```

Screenshots: Demo7.png (sunny pond), Demo6.png (aurora), Demo8.png (companion and cabin). Each thumbnail retains its source dimensions, framing, colors and content; only the title overlay pixels change. No cropping, retouching, scenery generation or UI removal. Original files are untouched.
