# MindSpace — consent and Presage camera UI concepts

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Preview proposals only; not implemented. No camera, microphone, Presage measurement session, or AI conversation was started.

Each image shows two separate screens: consent above, camera positioning/test after affirmative consent below. All displayed test states are illustrative. The player's live scene will be retained for implementation; additional scenery, signs, taglines, and presentation labels in generated artwork are not requested game changes.

- [A — Quiet Glass](A-Quiet-Glass.png): warm translucent panels, matching the approved pause menu.
- [B — Night Sanctuary](B-Night-Sanctuary.png): dark navy glass and ivory text, matching the approved main menu.
- [C — Guided Setup](C-Guided-Setup.png): two-stage onboarding, compact permission cards and camera-first layout.

Approval is required before implementation. Visual styles can be combined at the user's request.

## Implementation guardrails

Retain separate, default-off voice, camera and proactive-guidance choices; preserve clear data-sharing disclosures and session-only consent. Current camera-guided companion functionality requires voice; explain this dependency rather than implying unsupported independent camera-only gameplay. No capture starts from hovering, opening the menu, or selecting a permission toggle alone. Preserve camera switching, retry, back, and continue-without-camera paths. Entry with camera remains disabled until real fresh camera/position/pulse/breathing readiness passes existing criteria; do not simulate readings or percent-complete. Use one interactive permission control per row (generated concept B's duplicate-looking Off pill/Enable checkbox should become one control and one status). Retain detailed privacy disclosures, AI-not-clinician explanation, and companion choice in implementation.
