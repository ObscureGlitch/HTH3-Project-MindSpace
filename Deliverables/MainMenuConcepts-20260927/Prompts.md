# MindSpace main-menu concepts

Preview-only design proposals. Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Nothing has been implemented in Unity; user approval is required before implementation. The backgrounds are artistic interpretations of the existing room and garden, not exact Unity screenshots.

## A — Quiet Arrival

Use case: ui-mockup.
Asset type: high-fidelity desktop Unity game MAIN MENU design proposal, one full 16:9 landscape screen per image, approximately 1920x1080. This is an approval mockup, not a screenshot of an implemented feature.
Game identity: MindSpace, a peaceful exploration and AI-companion counseling game. Its actual environment is a modest single-storey cedar counseling cabin with dark sage gable roof and broad triangular attic glazing, warm off-white interior plaster, light wood flooring, cream armchairs and sofas with sage green pillows, houseplants, a wooden fenced low-poly green garden, flowers, trees and a reflective pond. Match this cohesive cozy stylized Unity aesthetic: softly shaded room furnishings with recognizably faceted garden foliage, not photographic luxury architecture, not a fantasy castle.
UI language: elegant readable serif title and primary labels, restrained neutral sans-serif supporting text. Smooth rounded corners where containers exist, subtle thin outlines, no heavy hard-edged panels. Refined accessible contrast and generous spacing. Keep most of the world visible. Exact game title "MindSpace". Exactly five menu actions: "Enter MindSpace", "Settings", "How to play", "Credits", "Exit". Small factual footer "Voice is optional". No continue/save-slot claims, no medical promises, no microphone or biometric dashboard, no heart rate, no gameplay HUD, no captions, no floating Unity gizmos, no cursor, no extra random text, no watermark or device frame. Do not reproduce the pause screen; this is the title screen before entering the game.
Concept A / QUIET ARRIVAL. Tiny presentation label at the far top-left "A / QUIET ARRIVAL".
Composition: a slim floating warm ivory frosted-glass menu occupying only the left 29% of screen with 6% outer margin and soft 24px rounded corners. It is transparent enough to see a suggestion of the interior behind it, not milky opaque. Right two-thirds present a beautiful seated-height three-quarter view of the actual welcoming therapy room: cream armchair and sofa with sage cushions, small round oak table, potted broadleaf plant, warm lamps, pale curtains and a window looking out into the low-poly garden. No people. Late afternoon warm natural window light; soft realistic ambient shading, quiet uncluttered composition.
UI: large deep-forest serif "MindSpace" near top of glass panel, small spaced subtitle "COUNSELING CENTER". Below, generous vertical rhythm for the five exact actions. "Enter MindSpace" is the only filled pill, muted sage with clear warm white text. Remaining four actions are static dark olive text, no bulky boxes. A fine short divider above Exit. Very tiny restrained footer "Voice is optional" near bottom of panel. Main menu title and all actions must be fully legible, carefully aligned and not clipped. This direction should feel like the same design family as an ivory-and-sage Quiet Glass pause menu.

Concept C / NIGHT SANCTUARY. Tiny presentation label at far top-left "C / NIGHT SANCTUARY".
Composition: wide ground-level view across the game's calm pond at blue hour/night. The cedar counseling cabin with glowing cream windows stands on right middle distance amid low-poly pines and gently faceted trees and flowers; wooden fence and softly painted distant hills enclose the garden. A detailed moon and sparse stars in the upper right, one subtle shooting star, graceful soft green/teal aurora curtains with faint rose-pink tips across the sky, believable not neon bands. Tiny low-contrast but readable "Voice is optional" bottom-left. No glass rectangle and no additional HUD. Mood is intimate, luminous, restful and immersive while typography stays crisp and very readable.
